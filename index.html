BAY OF BENGAL　 /　 AIR PATROL 01

Bengal
Shield

Fly your fighter over the island chain. Intercept the fighter patrol, clear the support flight, then silence the two island installations.

How to play
Quick start
  1. Choose your fighter. Select the J-10CE or MiG-29 before launching your level.
  2. Fly. Move the mouse to steer. Bring it back to the center to level out.
  3. Find a contact. Follow the amber marker. If it leaves the screen, follow the turn and altitude cues.
  4. Engage. Hold left-click for the cannon. Keep the marked contact near the reticle until the lock ring fills, then right-click for a missile.
Mouse & keyboard
Mouse
Steer in any direction. Farther from center gives a stronger response. The jet banks into turns. Hard turns and climbs cost speed, while leveling out or descending helps recover it. At full boost, allow room for a wider turn.
Arrow keys
↑ climb, ↓ descend, ← turn left, → turn right. Release to level out when the pointer is centered.
W / S · A / D
W lowers the nose, S raises it. A and D turn left and right.
Q / E
Make a small left or right heading correction.
Shift / Ctrl
Hold Shift to accelerate to 2,170 km/h in boost. Release to return to cruise, or hold Ctrl for the airbrake.
Left-click / Space
Fire the cannon. Hold for continuous fire.
Right-click / F
Launch one missile with a full target lock.
X
Release a flare burst to divert one incoming missile. The system recharges in 8 seconds of flight.
R / Tab
R selects the next contact. Tab cycles compact radar, expanded radar, and radar off.
C / Middle drag
C cycles chase, close chase, cockpit, target, and missile views. Hold the middle mouse button and drag to look around in chase view.
Esc / Ⅱ
Pause to read this guide, adjust settings, restart the level, or resume.
Touch controls

Tap Launch to enter landscape play. If your browser keeps its toolbar visible, the game still fills the available space.

Push the left stick up to climb, down to descend, or sideways to turn. Release to level out. Hold Boost to accelerate to 2,170 km/h or Gun to fire. Tap Target to select a contact and Missile once the lock ring fills.

Tap Flare during an incoming warning to divert one missile. Tap View to switch cameras, Radar to change radar size or hide it, and Ⅱ to pause. Drag on open sky to look around in chase view.

Radar, target lock & ammunition

Your jet is the triangle at the radar center. Green marks your wingman, amber marks your selected enemy, and pink marks other enemies. The compass shows heading, speed is in km/h, and altitude is in meters.

Bring the selected contact within 1,250 m and keep it near the nose until the ring fills and the lock tone sounds. The cannon can fire at any time while rounds remain.

You start with 120 rounds and 6 missiles. Every 12 seconds of flight restores up to 35 rounds and one missile. Clearing either of the first two phases adds up to two missiles, capped at six.

Health, warnings & repair

HULL shows your health percentage. The bar and HP count beside each visible enemy show its remaining health. LAUNCHING signals an enemy attack. The incoming alarm pulses faster as a missile gets closer.

After eight seconds without damage, REPAIRING appears. You recover one hull point every two seconds, up to 100%. A new hit restarts the delay. Climb when the terrain warning appears. Hitting the ground or water, or reaching zero hull, ends the sortie.

Levels, scoring & settings

Clear all contacts in three phases per level: fighters, a support flight, then island installations. Finish a level to unlock the next of six. You can retry after defeat or replay an unlocked level. Progress saves in this browser when local storage is available.

Aircraft are worth 500 points each and ground installations 400. Completing a level earns five bonus points per remaining hull point. Each new attempt starts with full hull and ammunition.

Choose Low, Medium, or High graphics quality before launch or while paused. Use Volume or Mute to adjust sound.
